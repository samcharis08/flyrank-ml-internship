Content Opportunity Ranking & Decision Support

Capstone Research Paper — FlyRank Content Portfolio Case Study

Abstract & Case Study Context

Context: Large digital content operations frequently manage thousands of published URLs where performance degrades silently or fails to capture expected search visibility. For content-driven platforms like FlyRank, manually auditing every page to improve click-through rates (CTR) and engagement is resource-prohibitive.

Objective: This paper presents an observational machine learning approach to rank high-impression, low-CTR content for targeted editorial review, framing model outputs strictly as decision-support tools rather than automated rewrite engines.

1. Question

Research Question: How can content performance metrics be used to prioritize high-impression, low-CTR pages for potential editorial review?

Decision Supported: Provides decision-support ranking for content strategists to identify pages deserving human review first, rather than attempting automated page rewrites.

2. Data

The analysis utilizes an anonymized release of 30,000 content performance records across 32 clients. Metrics track trailing 90-day performance (impressions, clicks, position, and days since last update). Exclusions were applied to non-visible pages to isolate realistic optimization candidates.

3. Methodology

The target label (opportunity) is defined by visible pages (position ≤ 20) with high impression volume (≥ 500) and low CTR (< 0.5%). We evaluated models using both random split and grouped-by-client validation designs to detect client-level data leakage.

4. Results (vs. Baseline)

Initial evaluation on a random split yielded high Precision@50 (0.920). However, evaluating across unseen client groups (grouped split) revealed a more realistic performance of 0.560, still substantially outperforming the observed base rate of 0.1844.

5. Limitations

  • Decision Support Only: Flagged pages indicate audit priority, not guaranteed performance improvements.
  • Client Generalization: The drop from random to grouped validation highlights that learned patterns vary across distinct client domains.
  • Observational Data: Single 90-day window without controlled A/B interventions.

6. Ranked Recommendations

Action queue generated with 800 flagged pages labeled under weak_ctr_visible for human editor triage. Strict no-go rules prohibit automated bulk rewrites or deleting pages without organic search audit.
